Mage Leveling 1–60

Updated Oct 26, 2026

Mage is one of the fastest classes to level, about 8% faster than average by LFCarry’s estimate. It conjures its own food and water, and slows and freezes enemies. In Forever, Frost gained Ice Lance and Fingers of Frost.

Spec: Frost · Talents 10–60 · Beta data

Which spec to level

Level Frost. This is the advice of Warcraft Tavern, Mobalytics and Icy Veins: Frostbolt slows, Frost Nova holds the enemy in place, and Ice Lance on a frozen target hits several times harder. Enemies rarely reach you. In the leveling tier list it is S.

Fire costs less mana but depends on crits. Arcane depends on procs and mana. According to Tavern, both specs are better in dungeons than in the open world.

  1. 1
    Before the fight

    Frost Armor and Arcane Intellect; Frost Armor can trigger Frostbite on an attacker.

  2. 2
    Opening

    Frostbolt from maximum range.

  3. 3
    In the fight

    Frostbolt; Ice Lance when the enemy is frozen or Fingers of Frost procs. If the enemy gets close, Frost Nova and step away; if it is on cooldown, Cone of Cold.

  4. 4
    Finishing

    with the wand: that way mana builds up for the next enemy.

Gather several enemies, freeze them (Frost Nova), step away, then Blizzard; Cone of Cold buys time to retreat, and Arcane Explosion finishes them off. Area damage costs a lot of mana, and in Forever it is weakened for leveling. In dungeons, don’t start before the tank.

Solo, duo, group

Before a fight, keep a stack of food and water. Always keep mana for Frost Nova and Blink: that is your way out of a bad fight. Get a wand as early as possible: Lesser Magic Wand from level 5 (Icy Veins).

Conjure food and water for your partner. Polymorph takes one enemy out of the fight while the two of you hit the other.

Blizzard and Arcane Explosion easily pull threat off the tank: start after them. From level 20, a teleport to a capital city; from 40, portals for the group.

Stats and gear

Mobalytics: spell power, Intellect (spell crit and mana), Spirit, Stamina. Get a wand as early as possible. Buy reagents: Rune of Teleportation and Light Feather for spells (Mobalytics). A Mana Gem and Evocation let you go longer without water.

Class profession pairings and camp objects are in the professions guide.

Questions

Which Mage spec should I level in WoW Forever?

Frost: Warcraft Tavern, Mobalytics and Icy Veins recommend it.

Can I level by area damage as in Classic?

You can, but in Forever area damage is weakened for leveling and expensive in mana. The main route is fighting one enemy at a time: Frostbolt, Ice Lance.

When does Ice Lance appear?

It is a Frost tree talent: Icy Veins takes it at level 20.

Where does a Mage get food and water?

It conjures them itself: water from level 4, food from level 6.

When do teleports and portals open?

A teleport to a capital city from level 20, portals for the group from 40.
