Alliance or Horde

Picker: race and class

Warcraft Tavern, Method

Choose a faction and role, look at the combinations and click a cell. Below you will see the race abilities and class roles, and your choice is copied with one button for the group chat.

Role
allowednewthe combination did not exist in Classicnot allowed

New combinations: dwarf shaman and gnome priest (Alliance), human hunter, and for the Horde orc mage, troll warlock and undead paladin. The Horde gained the paladin, the Alliance the shaman. The Windshaper Skyborne (Horde) can be a shaman, the High Order Skyborne (Alliance) a mage.
