      Play style

      Ping and queue

      Choose where you play from. Below are your WoW Forever region and what to do about high ping and in the login queue.

      High ping

      • Latency is shown in the tooltip of the game menu button: Home and World. World is the one that matters in combat.
      • Esc → Options → Network: untick “Optimize Network for Speed” and restart the game. per beta data
      • A VPN is the last resort. In the beta the servers were in the US only, and players from Europe were helped by a VPN with a US server. per beta data At launch Europe has its own region. If you still need a VPN, pick its server near the game region, not near you. A VPN does not guarantee lower latency.

      Login queue

      • Do not leave the queue: when you log in again you go to the back of it.
      • In the beta many players were disconnected after the queue and put at the back of a new one. per beta data If you are disconnected, log in again right away.
      • Heroic, Epic and Collection owners claim their name in advance, from Oct 27 to Nov 3: on launch day nobody takes it while you wait in the queue.

      Sources: Blizzard: rulesets instead of realms, Blizzard forums: stress test regions, Warcraft Tavern: VPN in the beta, Xbox Play: Optimize Network for Speed, Bolverk Games: beta queues.
