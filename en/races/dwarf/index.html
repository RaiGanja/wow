What is new in Forever

Warcraft Tavern

Compared with Classic. Blizzard may still change the numbers before release.

  1. New class: Shaman. The first Alliance shaman in the history of Classic.
  2. Earth Fury replaced Gun Specialization.
  3. Big Game Hunter replaced Frost Resistance.
  4. Fear Ward is now available to every priest, so a dwarf is no longer required.

Story and choice

Story

The dwarves of the Bronzebeard clan carved their capital right into the heart of a mountain. King Magni Bronzebeard searches for traces of ancient ancestors, and young dwarves begin their journey in snowy Dun Morogh.

tipStoneform is useful for tanks and in PvP.
