High ping
Latency is shown in the tooltip of the game menu button: Home and World. World is the one that matters in combat.
Esc → Options → Network: untick “Optimize Network for Speed” and restart the game.
A VPN is the last resort. In the beta the servers were in the US only, and players from Europe were helped by a VPN with a US server.
At launch Europe has its own region. If you still need a VPN, pick its server near the game region, not near you. A VPN does not guarantee lower latency.
Login queue
Do not leave the queue: when you log in again you go to the back of it.
In the beta many players were disconnected after the queue and put at the back of a new one.
If you are disconnected, log in again right away.
Heroic, Epic and Collection owners claim their name in advance, from 27.10 to 03.11: on launch day nobody takes it while you wait in the queue.

Americas and Oceania
Latin America plays in the Americas region.
Blizzard groups Oceania with the Americas in one region. Blizzard has not said whether there are servers in Oceania itself.
There is no region of your own. The nearest on the map are Taiwan and Korea. Pick the one with lower World latency.
Your region:
There are no realms: you choose a region and a ruleset, not a server. The region is chosen in Battle.net before you start the game.
Each region has its own characters and names. You can only play together within one region.
Blizzard has not said where the region servers are located.
