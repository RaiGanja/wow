What and when
The key dates before and after release.
Closed beta
Access was limited to owners of the Epic and Collection packs. The beta is temporarily closed; Blizzard says it will return.
Major beta patch
Racial abilities were reworked (weapon specializations replaced with crits) and almost all classes were tuned.
Early name reservation
Included in the Heroic, Epic and Collection packs. First come, first served; this is not early access to the game. Some sites say it starts on 23.10, so it is safer to check Blizzard’s page.
WoW Forever release
A subscription or game time is required. Forever is a permanent game alongside Retail and Classic, not a season.
Raids open
Barrow Deeps (10 players), Hyjal Summit (20), Onyxia’s Lair (40). Until then you have five weeks for leveling and dungeons.
Packs and prices
$29.99: unlocks the Skyborne race, its starting zone and early name reservation.
$59.99: the same plus beta access.
$79.99, on sale until 11.01.2027, with beta access.
The other eight races and all nine classes do not need to be bought.
Game modes
Normal, PvP and RP. Hardcore will launch later with its own rules.
You can only group within one mode. In PvP only one faction is available at character creation.
Agree in advance:
one faction, one mode, the same days for launch.

Each race has four abilities: two active and two passive. Some old abilities were replaced with new ones, resistances were removed.
Skyborne
is playable for both factions; the faction is chosen at creation. Both branches speak a common language, and the Zephras Isle starting zone is shared, with no enemies meeting there because of layers.
Legacy system
Progress for the whole Battle.net account. Points are earned from challenges and spent on perks separately on each character.
How to get points
The system unlocks with the first point: level 25 on a character, or 150 in a profession (not gathering), or exploring the whole map.
Each challenge gives 1 point. According to Blizzard there are 65 challenges in six categories: classes 27, professions 18, PvP 12, dungeons 3, raids 3, adventures 2.
Points apply to all non-Hardcore characters. Window hotkey: Y.
Limit at launch:
you can spend no more than 16 points on a character. The excess goes to the rewards track.
Rewards track (by number of points)
15: toy Holstered Replica Ironforge Air Rifle
25: pet Spectral Bear Cub
40: tabard Spectral Bear
55: mount Reins of the Spectral Bear
These are cosmetics and give no advantage in the game.

Example perks (ranks)
Professions
Working Overtime (5): skills increase faster. Bountiful Harvest (5): 20% more rare materials. Master Chef (5): chance of an extra dish. Bartering (2): vendor prices are 5% lower.
Adventures
Well Rested (5): rest accumulates faster and its cap is higher. Thrill of Adventure (5): health and mana for kills outside dungeons. Talented (5): talents from level 9 instead of 10. Field Guide (3): shorter cooldown of camp objects. Frequent Flier (1): flights are 50% cheaper.
Resources
Gourmand (3): food lasts 33% longer. Reinforce (5): 8% less durability loss. Diplomat (5): +2% reputation. Permanence (2): camp buffs last longer. Reagent Economy (1): class abilities need no purchased reagents.
Tip:
camp and experience perks (Well Rested, Field Guide, Permanence) are the most valuable first, plus Reagent Economy for classes with reagents. This is our assessment. One source mentions a 25-point limit per tree; Blizzard does not state a per-tree limit.
Camp and professions
A campfire made with the Cooking profession becomes a shared camp: you can rest nearby, get buffs and place objects of your professions.
How it works
The campfire is single-use: it takes a few seconds to build, burns for about 10 minutes, with a 5 minute cooldown to place.
A basic campfire is available without Cooking, with 3 objects. Cooking 140 gives 5 slots, 220 gives 10.
Each character places one object, with a 1 hour cooldown per object. Together you make a shared camp.
To get the buffs, sit (
) nearby for at least 1 minute. They stack into one buff, “Camp Benefits”.
Camp buffs are slightly weaker than class buffs and do not stack with them.
Bedroll and food
gives Well Rested, which stacks with camp buffs. It is obtained through a quest chain on both continents.
Most prepared food gives an experience bonus, which stacks with both the camp and the bedroll.
Legacy perks: Field Guide, Permanence, Reagent Economy improve the camp.

Group for a company of 6–8 players
Below are facts from the sources and our recommendations. Recommendations are marked separately; they are not official data.
What is known
Dungeons are designed for 5 players: a tank, a healer and three damage dealers.
Raids are for 10, 20 and 40 players. With six to eight friends, the 10-player Barrow Deeps can be filled with one or two acquaintances from a guild.
Warriors, Paladins and Druids can tank; Paladins, Priests, Shamans and Druids can heal.
Paladins and Druids can do all three roles. This is the most flexible pair for a small group.
Groups and raids receive class buffs: Kings from the paladin, shaman totems, the warrior’s Battle Shout, hunter and druid auras. Portals, food and water come from the mage.
Compiler’s recommendation
For 6:
a Warrior or Paladin (Protection) as tank, a Priest or Paladin (Holy) as healer, and four damage dealers: Mage, Hunter, Rogue, Warlock.
For 8:
two hybrid tanks (Paladin, Druid), two healers (Priest plus Shaman or Druid), and Mage, Hunter, Warlock and Shaman for damage. Then you can form two groups of 4–5 players or one of 5 and swap roles.
Take different classes: buffs of different classes stack, while identical ones overlap.
One of you takes Cooking for a campfire with 10 objects, the others pick professions for camp buffs. From 4 November to 9 December, level up together through dungeons.
Reserve names in the 27.10 – 03.11 window if you have a pack, and decide in advance who plays which class so you do not duplicate.
Leveling
By forecasts, Hunter and Mage level fastest (about 8% faster than average), Warrior slowest (about 12%), Druid and Priest slightly slower than average. These are beta estimates, the figures may change.
There is no level scaling: dungeons are for their own levels, and you cannot run them by “pulling up” a weaker friend.
Save up rest: the bedroll, Well Rested and food with an experience bonus stack.
You can set up camp right while leveling; five minutes for a campfire pays off with buffs for the group.

Before launch
until 04.11
Early name reservation 27.10 – 03.11 for owners of the Heroic, Epic and Collection packs. First come, first served.
You can reserve up to three characters per account. A character has a first name and a surname, and the pair must be unique within the region.
Pick three names with surnames, plus backups, before 27.10.
The first dungeons open at level 13.
Each character gets two primary professions plus all three secondary ones: Cooking, First Aid and Fishing.
Each profession places its own camp object with a buff similar to a class buff.
Split professions in advance: one of you takes Cooking for the campfire, the rest pick different ones so camp buffs do not repeat.
Legacy opens with your first point: level 25, 150 in a profession (not gathering) or exploring the whole map.
At launch a character can spend at most 16 points, the rest go to the reward track.
Agree on one faction and one mode: you can only group within a mode, and in PvP an account has one faction.
Build your roster in the group builder: a tank, a healer and different classes for buffs.
First days
levels 1–15
Skyborne start on Zephras Isle, levels 1–12.
Talents open at level 10, or at 9 with the Legacy perk Talented.
Most cooked food gives an experience bonus. It stacks with the camp and the Cozy Sleeping Bag.
Build up rest: log out at an inn or at a camp with a tent.
The basic campfire needs no Cooking and holds 3 objects. It burns for about 10 minutes, and you can place a new one after 5.
To get camp buffs, sit nearby (/sit) for at least a minute.
Pick up gathering from the first levels: herbs, ore and hides feed crafting, and 150 in a crafting profession opens Legacy.
No perks yet: the first point comes at level 25 or with 150 in a profession.
When Legacy opens, take experience and camp perks first: Well Rested, Talented, Field Guide.
Level in the same zone and set up camp at every stop: five minutes on a campfire pay off with buffs for the whole group.
Form your first group of five by level 13–15.
levels 15–40
Most dungeon experience comes from its quest chains. Repeat runs after them give noticeably less experience than questing in the world.
Every item that drops unlocks its transmog appearance for everyone who can wear it, even if someone else got the item.
Legacy opens at level 25.
Collect the dungeon quests as a group first and go in once everyone has them.
Cooking 140 gives 5 camp slots, 220 gives 10.
Each profession makes three camp objects, one per tier. Sources disagree on the skill needed for tiers 2 and 3.
If level 25 is still far off, take a crafting profession to 150: Legacy will open sooner.
Each challenge gives 1 point. There are 65: classes 27, professions 18, PvP 12, dungeons 3, raids 3, adventure 2.
Reagent Economy (no bought reagents for abilities) and Field Guide (shorter camp object cooldown) are worth it.
A dungeon is built for 5 players: a tank, a healer and three damage dealers.
There is no level scaling, so a friend who is far behind cannot join the dungeon.
Keep levels close: whoever falls behind catches up on quests, and you run dungeons together.
Road to 60
levels 40–60
A mount at level 40 (60% speed), a fast one at 60 (100%). Train with your race’s trainer.
Paladins and warlocks get their mount through class quests.
Honored with your own faction gives a 10% discount on riding training and mounts. Blizzard has not published exact prices, and sites disagree.
Save gold for level 40: riding will be your biggest expense on the road to 60.
Each profession has its own tier 2 and 3 camp objects. Engineering’s second object, Repair Bot, repairs gear.
Keep Cooking 220 in the group for a 10-object camp: by raid time there will be room for everyone.
At most 16 points per character, the rest go to the reward track: a toy at 15, a pet at 25, a tabard at 40, a mount at 55.
Raids open on 09.12: Barrow Deeps for 10 players, Hyjal Summit for 20, Onyxia’s Lair for 40.
For the 10-player raid, a group of 6–8 friends needs 1–2 more players: find them in your guild early.
60 and raids
On 09.12 Barrow Deeps (10 players), Hyjal Summit (20) and Onyxia’s Lair (40, Dustwallow Marsh) open.
Clearing each of the three raids gives a Legacy point.
While you gather people for the raid, run level 60 dungeons for gear.
Camp buffs are a bit weaker than class buffs and do not stack with them: Blessing of Kings gives 10% to all stats, the camp gives 8%.
Set up camp at the raid entrance: its buffs cover what your classes lack.
Challenges for level 60: dungeons 3, raids 3, PvP 12. PvP has ranks with a weekly cap and gear upgrades for Honor.
The same buffs from two players of one class overlap, different classes stack.
Build your raid roster in the group builder and post the link in chat.
